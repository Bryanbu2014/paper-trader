# 💸 Paper Trading Lab [![Open in Streamlit](https://static.streamlit.io/badges/streamlit_badge_black_white.svg)](https://paper-trading-lab.streamlit.app/)

![GitHub Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/Bryanbu2014/paper-trading-lab)
![Custom Release Date](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release_date-April_23,_2026-gre)

A clean, fast virtual stock trading terminal built with Python and Streamlit. 

Paper Trading Lab lets you practice stock trading using live market prices without risking real money. You get a virtual $100,000 starting balance, and all your trades, portfolio, and watchlists are saved to a cloud database.

## ✨ Features

- **🔒 Secure Login:** Private accounts so only you can access your portfolio.
- **☁️ Cloud Saves:** Your balance, trades, and watchlists are backed up to a Supabase database. You can close the app and pick up right where you left off.
- **📈 Live Data:** Real-time stock prices fetched using Yahoo Finance (`yfinance`).
- **💼 Portfolio Tracking:** Automatically calculates your total shares and average buy prices.
- **👀 Watchlist:** Add and track live prices for your favorite stocks.
- **📜 Trade Journal:** Keeps a full history of every buy and sell order.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **UI & Backend:** [Streamlit](https://streamlit.io/)
- **Database:** [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (PostgreSQL)
- **Market Data:** `yfinance`
- **Data Handling:** `pandas`
